Support

Help with using PDFPairViewer on macOS.

Need a hand? Email support@jmmartinezdev.com for questions, bug reports, or feature requests.

Getting started

  1. Open PDFPairViewer on your Mac.
  2. Choose a folder containing PDF files, or drag and drop a folder into the app.
  3. The app displays the first two PDFs side by side, in alphabetical order.
  4. Press the Down Arrow key to show the next pair of PDF files.
  5. Press the Up Arrow key to return to the previous pair.
  6. Use the toolbar controls to zoom in, zoom out, or return to the default zoom.

Frequently asked questions

Do my documents leave my Mac?

No. The app processes PDFs locally. Your documents are not uploaded to the developer.

How are files ordered?

The app displays supported PDFs alphabetically by filename, two at a time.

What if I only see one PDF?

Check that the selected folder contains at least two PDF files. Other file types aren't shown as PDFs.

Does the app highlight differences automatically?

PDFPairViewer is designed for side-by-side visual inspection. Automatic difference highlighting is not described as a current feature.

Contact support

For bugs, general feedback, and feature requests, contact support@jmmartinezdev.com.

If you're reporting a problem, include your macOS version, app version, and a description of the steps to reproduce it. Please do not email confidential PDF documents.
