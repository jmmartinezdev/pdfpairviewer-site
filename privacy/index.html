Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026 · PDFPairViewer for macOS

In short: PDFPairViewer processes your PDFs locally. The application does not upload your documents or collect analytics.

Who is responsible?

PDFPairViewer is developed by José María Martínez Muñoz, based in Spain. For privacy questions, contact support@jmmartinezdev.com.

How the app handles your documents

PDFPairViewer displays PDF files from folders you choose on your Mac. The documents and their contents are processed locally on your device and are not uploaded to servers operated by the developer. The developer does not receive your PDF contents.

The app may store application settings locally on your Mac to maintain preferences.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

The application does not integrate analytics, advertising, user-tracking, or third-party crash-reporting services. It does not create user accounts or request personal details to use its PDF viewing functionality.

Data sharing and retention

The application does not transmit document contents or usage data to developer-controlled servers. As a result, the developer does not hold such data on a server for sharing or deletion.

When you contact support

If you voluntarily email support, the developer receives your email address, message, and any information you choose to include. This is used to respond to your request, based on the legitimate interest in providing support (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Support messages are retained only as necessary to handle the request and applicable legal obligations.

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or object to its processing, where applicable, by emailing support@jmmartinezdev.com. You may also lodge a complaint with your relevant data protection authority, such as the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

This website

This static website is hosted using GitHub Pages. It does not include developer-added analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. GitHub may process technical information associated with visitors' requests to host and protect the service, as explained in GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated. The updated version will be published here with a revised date.
